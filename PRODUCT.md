# Velvet UI product context

## Product

Velvet UI is a proprietary React interface library for native-feeling sheets,
compositions, and toasts. It is distributed in compiled form through npm.

## Users

- Independent React developers choosing a license for their own work.
- Small teams with no more than two people using Velvet UI.
- Engineering and procurement leads licensing Velvet UI for one organization.
- Developers evaluating exact commercial rights before they buy.

## Purpose

Help developers ship tactile, accessible interfaces without rebuilding motion,
gesture, focus, and stacking behavior. The purchase experience must make the
license grant as understandable as the price.

## Brand personality

Tactile, precise, calm, and confident. Velvet should feel crafted and modern,
not loud, luxurious, or salesy.

## Design principles

1. Practice what the product sells. Licensing and checkout use Velvet sheets.
2. Disclose before persuading. Rights, limits, tax, refunds, and seller roles are
   visible before checkout.
3. One decision at a time. Start with the tier; use a nested sheet for details,
   acceptance, and the final action.
4. Make trust tangible. Use exact labels, restrained hierarchy, and clear status.
5. Prevent accidents, not escape. Disable backdrop and swipe dismissal while
   keeping a visible Done action, Back actions, and standard Escape behavior.

## Anti-references

- Generic four-card SaaS pricing grids.
- Countdown timers, fake scarcity, or manipulative urgency.
- Hidden qualifications in low-contrast fine print.
- Decorative faux-legal clutter and dense walls of text.
- Keyboard traps or dialogs without a clear exit.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful
dialog titles and descriptions, reduced-motion behavior, and readable contrast.

## Commercial model

- Free: non-commercial use, unlimited projects, reasonable-efforts support.
- Individual: USD 20 plus tax, one-time, at most two authorized users.
- Organization: USD 129 plus tax, one-time, one named organization.
- Codebase access: separate written agreement; contact sales.
- Paid perpetual access includes every future public version, without a promise
  that new versions, availability, or support will continue indefinitely.
- Dodo Payments is the merchant of record and generates the paid license key.
