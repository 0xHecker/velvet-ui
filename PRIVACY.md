operating Velvet UI (**“Velvet UI,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**), collects,

Cloudflare, npm, or another third party collects for its own purposes under its
own privacy notice.

## 2. Personal data we may collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

We may collect:

- your name, email address, organization, role, and contact details;
- support requests, license questions, bug reports, feedback, correspondence,
  and attachments you choose to send;
- information needed to identify the relevant Project, environment, package
  version, browser, device, or error; and
- information you provide when discussing Unminified Codebase access, custom
  terms, or another business arrangement.

Do not send credentials, private keys, payment-card details, unnecessary
personal data, or third-party confidential information.

### 2.2 Purchase and license information

Dodo Payments may provide us with information needed to fulfil and support a
purchase, such as:

- purchaser and billing name, email address, country, and organization;
- purchased product and tier, price, currency, tax status, and Order reference;
- payment, refund, dispute, and chargeback status;
- invoice or transaction identifiers; and
- License Key, entitlement, activation, and revocation information.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or card security code.
Payment credentials are collected and processed by Dodo Payments and its
payment providers.

### 2.3 Website and technical information

When you access the website, our hosting and security providers may process
technical information such as:

- Internet Protocol address;
- browser and device type;
- operating system;
- requested page or file, referring page, and request time;
- approximate region derived from an Internet Protocol address; and
- diagnostic, performance, error, abuse-prevention, and security-event data.

The documentation interface may use browser session storage to remember a
navigation scroll position during the browser session. That value remains on
your device and can be cleared through browser controls.

As of this Notice's effective date, the Velvet UI website does not use
third-party advertising cookies or behavioural advertising trackers. If that
changes, we will update this Notice and obtain consent where required.

### 2.4 Public GitHub issues

If you submit an issue to the public Velvet UI support repository, your GitHub
username, profile information, issue content, comments, timestamps, and any
attachments will be public. GitHub processes that information under its own
terms and privacy statement.

Do not post personal data, credentials, customer information, proprietary code,
or security details in a public issue. Send a non-public report to
<msshanmukh@gmail.com> instead.

We may use personal data to:

1. operate, deliver, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website,
   documentation, and Software;
2. verify purchases, identify the licensed tier and Licensee, administer
   License Keys, and provide future public versions;
3. provide technical, billing-coordination, license, privacy, and customer
   support;
4. respond to inquiries, feedback, complaints, and legal notices;
5. investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, License Key exposure, and
   violations of the Software License Agreement or Terms and Conditions;
6. improve the Software, documentation, support, and user experience;
7. maintain business, tax, accounting, transaction, and legal records;
8. establish, exercise, or defend legal claims and enforce agreements; and
9. comply with law, court orders, regulatory requests, sanctions, and other
   legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process personal data as
necessary to perform or take steps toward a contract, comply with legal
obligations, pursue legitimate interests such as security and support, or act
with consent. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the impact on
your rights and use data only where those interests are not overridden.

## 4. When we share personal data

We may share personal data only as reasonably necessary with:

- **Dodo Payments and its payment partners**, for orders, tax, invoices,
  License Keys, fraud prevention, refunds, and disputes;
- **Cloudflare and infrastructure providers**, for website delivery, security,
  logging, and performance;
- **GitHub**, when you choose to use the public support repository;
- **professional advisers**, such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, insurers,
  and security consultants, subject to appropriate duties of confidentiality;
- **government authorities, courts, regulators, and law enforcement**, when
  disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights,
  safety, and security; and
- **a successor or transaction participant**, in connection with a proposed or
  completed merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of the
  Velvet UI business or assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not use personal data for
third-party behavioural advertising or knowingly share it for cross-context
behavioural advertising.

Velvet UI is operated from India. Service providers and people with whom we
work may process data in India, the United States, the European Economic Area,
or other countries where they operate. Those countries may have different data
protection laws from your country.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we
will use an approved contractual mechanism or another lawful safeguard. Dodo
Payments and other independent controllers are responsible for safeguards
applicable to transfers they make for their own purposes.

## 6. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described in this Notice, including:

- purchase, Order, License Key, and entitlement records for the life of the
  license and afterward as needed for accounting, fraud prevention, and legal
  claims;
- tax, invoice, payment-status, and business records for the period required by
  applicable law;
- support correspondence until the matter is resolved and for a reasonable
  period afterward to maintain support history and handle follow-up disputes;
- security, diagnostic, and server records according to operational need and
  provider retention settings; and
- records relevant to a dispute, investigation, or legal hold until the matter
  and applicable limitation periods end.

Public GitHub content may remain available under GitHub's retention practices
and repository history even after an issue is closed. You may contact us to
request removal or redaction, but Git history, legal requirements, and copies
made by others may limit what can be removed.

When personal data is no longer needed, we will delete, anonymize, or securely
isolate it, subject to technical backup cycles and legal obligations.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures
designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration,
loss, misuse, or disclosure. Those measures may include access limitation,
reputable hosted infrastructure, encrypted transport, credential protection,
software updates, and incident response.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible
for protecting your email, GitHub account, devices, and License Key and for not
sending sensitive information through public support channels.

If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will investigate and provide
notices to affected people and authorities where applicable law requires.

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have the
right to:

- ask whether we process your personal data and obtain access to it;
- correct incomplete or inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive portable data where applicable;
- withdraw consent for future processing where processing relies on consent;
- nominate or authorize another person where applicable law permits; and
- complain to an applicable data protection or consumer authority.

To make a request, email <msshanmukh@gmail.com> with enough information for us
to identify you and the data involved. We may request proportionate

You can clear local session storage, cookies, and cached files through your

support responses that you request.
